E-INVOICE Oct 16th, 2026

Introduction of e-Invoicing Facility for Recipient Generated Self Invoices under RCMDOM and Import of Services (RCMIMP)

Taxpayers liable to pay tax under Reverse Charge Mechanism can now report recipient-generated tax invoices on the e-Invoice portal (IRP) for domestic and imported services with immediate effect.

RETURNS Active Alert Oct 13th, 2026

Annual Returns in the form of GSTR-9/9C for FY 2025-26

The facility to file Annual Return in Form GSTR-9 and Reconciliation Statement in Form GSTR-9C for the Financial Year 2025-26 is now enabled on the GST Common Portal for all regular taxpayers.

OTHERS Oct 13th, 2026

FAQs in relation to recommendations of the 57th GST Council Meeting

Terms of Use & Legal Disclaimer

This application is an educational simulation and mock portal designed for demonstration of statutory GST compliance, Form GSTR-1, GSTR-2B, and GSTR-3B return processing in India.

Privacy & Data Handling

No real taxpayer credentials or actual bank account balances are processed. All data calculations and statements execute strictly on client-side vanilla JavaScript.

Security Architecture

Simulating 256-bit TLS encryption, ISO 27001 compliance standards, digital signature token integration (emSigner), and multi-factor Electronic Verification Code (EVC) authentication.
